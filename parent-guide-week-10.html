This Week at a Glance

A new unit starts this week. Your student meets three Spanish expeditions that reached Florida between 1513 and 1539. Ponce de León named the land La Florida. Narváez’s expedition fell apart, and only four men survived. De Soto marched more than 600 soldiers across the Southeast looking for cities of gold that weren’t there. Then your student asks why they came, using a handwritten order from the king of Spain.

History skill
Order and reasons. Put the expeditions on a timeline, then name the three motives: God (religion), Gold (wealth), and Glory (land and honor for Spain).
Virtue this unit
Courage, seen two ways. It took courage to cross the ocean. The lessons also ask what that courage cost the people who already lived here.

“Their arrival was not a discovery; it was a meeting between strangers and the nations who had been living on this land for thousands of years.”Key idea from Lesson 4.04.01

Good to know: Ponce de León wasn’t looking for a Fountain of Youth. That story was added later. The lessons are also honest that de Soto’s army spread disease and took food by force. Two questions ask for your student’s own opinion, so there’s no single right answer, just a reason.

4.04.02Lesson 2 Why They Sailed: God, Gold, and Glory
Spain wanted to spread the Catholic faith, find gold like it had in Mexico and Peru, and claim land before France and England could. These reasons explain why expeditions kept coming after disasters, and why first contact was so often violent.
Source: a Spanish royal orderVirtue: Courage		 Source Study Response (4–6 sentences): what Spain really hoped to gain, and whether the explorers’ courage was used well.

Ask at Home

Better than “How was school?”
  1. Who were the three explorers, in order?Listen for: Ponce de León in 1513, Narváez in 1528, de Soto in 1539.
  2. Was Ponce de León looking for the Fountain of Youth?Listen for: no. He wanted new land and resources for Spain. That story came later.
  3. What do God, Gold, and Glory mean?Listen for: spreading religion, finding wealth, and winning land and honor for Spain.
  4. The explorers were brave. Was their courage used for something good?Listen for: an answer that sees both sides. The trip took courage, but it brought harm to the people already living here.

Try at Home

  • Spanish names. La Florida means “the flowery land.” Find other Spanish place names on a Florida map.

See It in Florida

Optional
⛵

De Soto National Memorial (Bradenton, on Tampa Bay). A national park site near where de Soto’s army came ashore in 1539. Historians still debate the exact spot. It has walking trails and, in some seasons, living-history demonstrations. Check the park calendar before you go.
