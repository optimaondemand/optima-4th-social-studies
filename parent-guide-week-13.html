This Week at a Glance

Unit 6 opens. Your student studies why Spain sailed for Florida (gold, land to claim, and spreading the Catholic faith) and the first three Spanish expeditions. Then they follow the chain of events in 1565 that made St. Augustine the oldest European-founded city in the 50 states that people have lived in every year since. Both lessons keep the Timucua and other first peoples in the story, because this was already their home.

History skill
Sequence and cause and effect. Putting five events from 1564–1565 in order, and explaining how one led to the next.
Virtue this week
Courage, on every side. Sailors crossing an ocean, French settlers under attack, and Timucua people facing strangers in their homeland.

Good to know: Lesson 4.06.01 mentions that new diseases like smallpox made whole villages sick, and that there was fighting on both sides. Lesson 4.06.02 includes Spain capturing the French fort in 1565. Both are told briefly, without graphic detail.

Five events in a chain: France builds Fort Caroline (1564), Spain’s king sends Menéndez, St. Augustine is founded on September 8, 1565, Spain captures the French fort, and the town survives, later building the stone Castillo de San Marcos. Ask at Home

Better than “How was school?”
  1. What three things pulled Spain toward Florida?Listen for: gold and riches, land to claim, and spreading the Catholic faith.
  2. How did Florida get its name?Listen for: Ponce de León named it La Florida in 1513: “the flowery land,” and he arrived near Easter, called Pascua Florida.
  3. Why did Spain start St. Augustine in 1565? What happened just before?Listen for: the French built Fort Caroline in 1564, so Spain sent Menéndez to push them out.
  4. Whose land was St. Augustine built on?Listen for: the Timucua people’s.

What Gets Turned In

  • Each lesson: 2–3 evidence questions, one longer writing piece, and a Historian’s Sentence, submitted in Canvas

Done well looks like: events in the right order, with sequence words (“first,” “then,” “after that”) and one clear “because.”

🏡

Try at Home

5 minutes
  • Name hunt. Find Florida places with Spanish names (St. Augustine, Boca Raton, Cape Canaveral). What might they mean?
  • Five in a row. Tell the story of your morning in five steps. Which step caused the next one?
  • How old? St. Augustine began in 1565. How many years ago was that?
